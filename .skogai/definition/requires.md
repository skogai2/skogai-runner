# requires

`requires` is a [[state]] key that lists [[name]]s. a thing can't be
understood or run without what it requires, and nothing else.

a requirement names what is needed, never who [[provides]] it.

following `requires` from a thing until nothing new turns up gives its
closure: everything it stands on, and the most it can ever need in
[[context]].
